Orlan's Minister for Trade, Teodor Vance, told reporters in Velmora that textile exporters would be among the first beneficiaries of the trade partnership.

Vance said Orlan's parliamentary trade committee would begin reviewing the agreement in December 2026. He did not give a date for a ratification vote.

Kestrian textile manufacturers' association chair Ruth Okafor said members were concerned about competition from Orlan.
