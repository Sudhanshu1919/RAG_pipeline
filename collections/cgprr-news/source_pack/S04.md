The Board of the Coastal Development Bank approved a USD 450 million loan to the Velmora Port Authority to finance the construction of Berth 7.

The loan has a tenor of 20 years, including a five-year grace period.

Berth 7 is designed to add capacity of 0.8 million TEU per year. Construction is scheduled to begin in the first quarter of 2027.
