The Monetary Policy Committee of the Central Bank of Kestria decided by a vote of 5 to 2 to keep the policy repo rate unchanged at 6.25%.

Headline consumer price inflation was 5.1% in October 2026, above the Bank's target of 4%.

The Committee projects inflation to average 4.7% in 2027.

The next meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee is scheduled for 17 December 2026.
