The Port of Velmora handled 2.4 million TEU in FY2025, up 12% from 2.14 million TEU in FY2024.

Bulk cargo volumes fell 3% to 18.6 million tonnes, mainly due to lower coal imports.

Average vessel turnaround time improved to 1.9 days in FY2025 from 2.3 days in FY2024.

The Authority's capital plan includes the construction of Berth 7, a deep-water container berth, and maintenance dredging of the main channel. The Authority has not published a completion date for the dredging work.

The Port Authority employed 4,350 staff at the end of FY2025.
