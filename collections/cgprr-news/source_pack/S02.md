Tariff lines covered by sector: industrial goods 640 lines; processed food 310 lines; textiles 250 lines. Total covered: 1,200 lines.

Phase-down schedule: the 25% reduction is applied in five equal annual steps of 5 percentage points of the base tariff, starting in the first year of entry into force.

Rules of origin: goods must contain at least 40% regional value content to qualify for reduced tariffs.

Excluded sectors: agricultural commodities, arms and defence equipment, and precious metals.
