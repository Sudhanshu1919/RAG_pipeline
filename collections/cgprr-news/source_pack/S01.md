The Republic of Kestria and the Federation of Orlan signed the Kestria–Orlan Trade Partnership Agreement (KOTP) in Velmora on 3 November 2026. The agreement was signed by Kestria's Minister of Commerce, Ilsa Marrow, and Orlan's Minister for Trade, Teodor Vance.

The agreement covers 1,200 tariff lines across industrial goods, processed food and textiles. Tariffs on covered lines will be reduced by 25% in phases over five years, beginning on the date the agreement enters into force.

The Ministry of Commerce projects that bilateral trade between the two countries could reach USD 18 billion by 2030, compared with USD 11.2 billion in 2025.

The agreement enters into force only after ratification by both parliaments. Kestria's National Assembly is scheduled to debate the agreement in January 2027. Ratification by Orlan's parliament is pending.

The agreement does not include provisions on agricultural subsidies, which both sides agreed to discuss in a separate track.
