The Kestria Navy commissioned the offshore patrol vessel KNS Arvel at Velmora Naval Base on 10 November 2026.

KNS Arvel was built by Halden Shipyard. She is 92 metres long and has a crew of 64.

KNS Arvel is the third offshore patrol vessel built domestically for the Kestria Navy, following KNS Brisk and KNS Corran.

The vessel will be assigned to the Eastern Fleet and will conduct maritime surveillance and search-and-rescue missions.
