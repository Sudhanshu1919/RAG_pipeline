The Central Bank of Kestria held its policy repo rate at 6.25% on Thursday, in a unanimous decision.

Inflation rose to 5.1% in October from 5.4% in September.

Food prices rose 7.2% year on year.

Governor Mara Elling signalled that the bank would cut rates at its December meeting.

The bank projects inflation will average 4.7% in 2027.

Elling said it was too early to quantify the effect of the Orlan trade agreement.
