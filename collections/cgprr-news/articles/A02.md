The Port of Velmora handled 2.4 million TEU in FY2025, a jump of 18% on the previous year.

Turnaround time for vessels fell to 1.9 days.

The Coastal Development Bank has approved a USD 450 million loan for a new deep-water container berth, Berth 7, which will add 0.8 million TEU of annual capacity.

Port chief executive Daniel Ruiz said dredging of the main channel would be completed by March 2027.

Construction of Berth 7 is due to start in early 2027.

The port employs more than 5,000 people.
