The Kestria Navy commissioned KNS Arvel, its first domestically built offshore patrol vessel, on 10 November 2026.

Built by Halden Shipyard, the 92-metre vessel carries a crew of 64.

The ship was delivered six months ahead of schedule.

KNS Arvel will join the Western Fleet.

Defence analysts said the vessel cost about USD 120 million.
